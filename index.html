Common questions

Private video playback, explained.

Does Screening Room import videos into a private vault?

No. Screening Room is designed to encrypt and play files in the storage location you choose, rather than maintaining a separate media vault inside the app.

Can I use it with iCloud Drive or a NAS?

Yes. Screening Room works with user-selected locations exposed through the iOS Files system, including iCloud Drive, NAS connections, and compatible third-party file providers.

Will videos appear in Files Recents after I watch them?

Media viewed through Screening Room is not added as a standard file-preview entry in Files Recents. The encrypted file itself may still be visible in Files according to the location and Files app behavior.

Does Screening Room upload my files?

Not during ordinary use. File encryption, decryption, and playback are performed on your device, and the app does not automatically upload your media to developer-controlled servers. Voluntary support and recovery requests are described in the privacy policy.

Is Screening Room a zero-knowledge encryption service?

No. Each .efl file contains developer-managed escrow recovery data. A party with the corresponding developer private key may be technically able to recover the file key after obtaining the encrypted file. See the privacy policy for full details.
